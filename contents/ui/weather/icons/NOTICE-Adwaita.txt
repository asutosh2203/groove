sunrise-glyph.svg and sunset-glyph.svg are copies of daytime-sunrise-symbolic.svg and daytime-sunset-symbolic.svg from the
Adwaita icon theme (GNOME), offered under CC-BY-SA-3.0 OR LGPL-3.0-only and used here under LGPL-3.0-only. The licence text
is LICENSES/LGPL-3.0-only.txt and the REUSE entry is REUSE.toml, both at the repository root. They are the sunrise and
sunset glyphs of the Weather widget (D-55), used only when the icon theme has no daytime-sunrise-symbolic or
daytime-sunset-symbolic of its own. They are renamed on purpose: Kirigami resolves a file URL whose name matches a theme icon
through the theme instead of loading the file.
